# SECOM fail prediction

Generated by `make secom-model`; don't edit by hand. Code: `src/waferlens/ml/`; runs and the
registered model (`secom-fail-predictor` v2) in MLflow; scores and sensor
importance in `secom_scores` / `secom_sensor_importance` for Grafana. Data card:
[docs/data/secom.md](data/secom.md).

**On the last 30% of runs, never seen during training or model selection, the chosen model
reaches PR-AUC 0.065 (95% CI 0.054–0.091) against
0.055 for chance (1.2x). The same model on a random split scores
0.177: a time-blind evaluation would overstate it 2.7x.**
The 95% interval includes chance: forward in time, the model is not reliably better than a random ranking.

missing values as they are ("native").

## Holdout (scored once)

| Model | PR-AUC (95% CI) | ROC-AUC | Recall at 5% false alarms: forward | hindsight | Recall at 10% false alarms: forward | hindsight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lgbm · native · none | 0.065 (0.054–0.091) | 0.579 | 0% (at 2.3%) | 0% | 0% (at 3.6%) | 4% |
| logreg · median · mutual_info | 0.081 (0.061–0.134) | 0.613 | 0% (at 0.2%) | 12% | 0% (at 1.1%) | 12% |
| *chance* | *0.055* | *0.500* | *5%* | *5%* | *10%* | *10%* |

The chosen configuration scored 0.129 in walk-forward CV and 0.065 on the
holdout: picking the best of 17 configurations on 78
training fails selects partly for luck, so the winner's CV score is optimistic. The logistic baseline scored higher on the holdout (0.081); switching to it now would be choosing the model on the test set, so the choice stands and the comparison is reported instead.

*Forward*: the alarm threshold is set on the training period's walk-forward scores for that
false-alarm budget and applied unchanged to the holdout; the false-alarm rate it actually
produced is in brackets. *Hindsight*: the best recall at that budget with the threshold set
on the holdout itself, an upper bound no deployment gets.

The 10% threshold from the training period produced a 3.6% false-alarm
rate on the holdout and caught 0% of fails. When the process drifts, the
score distribution moves with it, and last month's threshold no longer means the same alarm
rate: a deployed model needs its threshold recalibrated on recent runs, or a rank-based
alarm (the top k% of runs each week).

but can't say what they measure. The last column is the train-period difference between
fails and passes in standard deviations.

## Limitations

- **26 holdout fails**: the confidence interval is wide, and a different
  holdout boundary would move the numbers.
- **One line, three months**: nothing here says how this generalises to another line.
- **Ranking aid, not a gate**: at ROC-AUC 0.58 the scores can order runs
  for inspection, but not pass or fail them. The baseline (logreg · median · mutual_info) reaches PR-AUC
  0.081.
